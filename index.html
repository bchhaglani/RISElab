Overview

We explore how everyday devices can help us better understand and improve our health.

Much of what shapes our health happens between clinical visits—in our daily activities, habits, and environments. We leverage ambient signals around us to help fill these gaps and provide meaningful insights into our holistic health, while protecting privacy and supporting human agency.

Our research combines ubiquitous computing, signal processing, machine learning, and human-centered computing across three connected directions:

  • Responsible sensing and AI: understanding what sensitive information sensors reveal, designing privacy-aware systems with domain-adaptive protection, and advancing trustworthy AI, explainability, and evaluation.
  • Mobile and wearable sensing for health: assessing voice health, posture, and everyday behaviors, and supporting personalized feedback and interventions.
  • Healthy environments: monitoring environmental exposure and indoor air, mapping environments, and predicting health risks to support environmental health.

We are building our team! We welcome interest from PhD, master’s, and undergraduate students.

Research

Selected publications →
01

Responsible Sensing and AI

How can sensing systems provide useful insights while respecting privacy and agency? We investigate unintended information leakage from audio and other sensor representations, develop privacy-aware methods with domain-adaptive protection, and advance trustworthy AI through explainability and evaluation.

FeatureSense · Audio privacyArtiFree · Trustworthy speech AI
02

Mobile and Wearable Sensing for Health

We use acoustic and wearable signals to understand health beyond clinical settings. Our interests include voice health, accessible speech technology, posture assessment, and personalized interventions that fit into everyday life.

NeckCare · Hearable-based posture sensingDysarthria assessment · Privacy-aware speech sensing
03

Healthy Environments

We develop ubiquitous sensing methods to monitor environmental exposure, assess indoor air, and map the environments around us. By combining sensor measurements with machine learning and knowledge of physical systems, we aim to predict health risks and support environmental health.

FlowSense · Ventilation monitoringAeroSense · Aerosol emission sensing
