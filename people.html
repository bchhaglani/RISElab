LAB DIRECTOR

Bhawana Chhaglani

Assistant Professor
College of Information Sciences and Technology
Penn State University

My research focuses on privacy-aware sensing systems for health and wellness. I combine mobile and wearable sensing, machine learning, and human-centered computing to study human health, healthy environments, and responsible sensing.

I earned my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Massachusetts Amherst, advised by Prashant Shenoy and co-advised by Jeremy Gummeson.
