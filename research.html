OUR RESEARCH

From everyday signals
to meaningful insights.

We build sensing systems that help people understand their health and environments, with privacy and agency built into the process.

01

Responsible sensing & AI

Sensing systems that earn trust.

We study what sensitive information everyday sensors reveal and develop methods to protect privacy, evaluate information leakage, and improve the reliability of AI for sensing.

Audio privacy · Leakage auditing · Trustworthy AI

FeatureSense

Investigating the utility and privacy of acoustic features for everyday sensing.

CLEAR

Evaluating speech-content leakage to inform privacy-aware audio processing.

02

Mobile & wearable health

Health insights beyond the clinic.

We explore acoustic and wearable signals to assess voice health and everyday behaviors, with an emphasis on accessible technology, personalized feedback, and interventions.

Voice health · Wearables · Accessible speech technology

DysSense

Exploring privacy-aware acoustic assessment of dysarthric speech.

Everyday health sensing

Investigating wearable and acoustic sensing for health and well-being.

03

Healthy environments

Understanding the spaces we share.

We combine ubiquitous sensing, machine learning, and knowledge of physical systems to understand ventilation, environmental exposure, and factors that influence airborne infection risk.

Ventilation · Environmental exposure · Sensor fusion

FlowSense

Using acoustic sensing to study indoor ventilation.

AeroSense

Exploring acoustic approaches to sensing parameters relevant to airborne transmission.
